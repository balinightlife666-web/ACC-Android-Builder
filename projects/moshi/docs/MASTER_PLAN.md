# MOSHI — MASTER PLAN v1.1

Status: ACTIVE / PHASE 2 CHAT CORE

## Vision
MOSHI is a single all-in-one communication app for personal chat, groups, communities, business selling, and AI-assisted communication.

## Core differentiator
**MOSHI KNOWS WHAT YOU MISSED.**

AI is part of communication management, not a separate novelty chatbot.

## Product pillars
1. Personal Messaging
2. Groups & Communities
3. Business & Commerce
4. AI Communication Intelligence
5. Calls & Multi-device (post-MVP)

## One-app business rule
There will not be a separate MOSHI Business APK. A user can enable Business Mode inside the same MOSHI account and can later manage multiple business identities without reinstalling or switching apps.

## Build order
Phase 0 — Foundation
Phase 1 — Identity/Auth
Phase 2 — Realtime Chat Core
Phase 3 — Media & Messaging Tools
Phase 4 — Groups
Phase 5 — Business Mode MVP
Phase 6 — MOSHI AI Summary
Phase 7 — Communities/Channels
Phase 8 — Voice/Video Calls
Phase 9 — Stories
Phase 10 — Multi-device/Web
Phase 11 — Bots/Mini Apps

## Non-goals for early MVP
No crypto, marketplace feed, advertising network, large video conferencing, custom cryptography, or separate business app.
