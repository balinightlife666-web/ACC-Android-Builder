# LOST & FOUND: NIGHT SHIFT — M5-C COLLECTIBLE 3D QUALITY PASS v1

Status: ACTIVE
Scope: visual/presentation-only upgrade for serialized collectible 3D previews and station showcase models.

## Runtime motivation
M5-B.2 established the five-slot personal trophy rack. Runtime QC shows the rack itself reads well, but several collectible models (especially teddy, formal shoe, passport and small object classes) still read as simple primitive placeholders. M5-C raises the collectible visual quality without changing collection ownership/economy/gameplay.

## Visual direction
- Roblox in-engine geometry/materials only.
- NO generated images, external textures, decals or image assets.
- Preserve recognizable silhouette and collection identity.
- Add believable construction details: seams, edge trim, latches, handles, zipper lines, soles, tongues/laces, page blocks, lens rings, stitching, wheels, vents, control panels, etc.
- Keep mobile-safe part counts; detail should read from the five-slot showcase distance.
- Rarity may receive restrained material/accent treatment, but no glitter/particle spam and no gameplay implication.
- SECRET/ANOMALY may look more distinctive while remaining consistent with Season 1 mystery canon; visual decoration must not reveal final mystery explanations.

## v1 coverage
Upgrade the shared CollectionPreviewFactory used by the collection/index and public showcase, covering all current base families:
- hardcase suitcase
- vintage suitcase
- backpack
- cardboard parcel
- teddy bear
- camera lens
- evidence/boarding tag
- passport
- toy train
- power adapter
- formal shoe
- stitched name patch
- paperback
- mass readout

Collection-specific details are allowed for current S1 serialized entries, but IDs, names, rarity, serial prefixes and edition remain unchanged.

## Gameplay/economy/canon firewall
M5-C MUST NOT change:
- case generation, evidence, correct decisions, difficulty or grading;
- Season 1 mystery canon or explanations;
- Credits, XP or Station Shop prices/ownership;
- drop rates/chances or mystery chance;
- Collection Index discovery history;
- inventory ownership or item granting;
- instanceId, serial, serial number, mint counter, edition or provenance;
- TradeService transfer/validation rules;
- five-slot showcase selection/persistence/trade cleanup;
- station ownership/isolation;
- SCAN/TAG/OPEN/DECIDE validation;
- EN/ID localization or decision colors.

## Runtime acceptance
1. Five-slot rack remains unchanged and stable on Station A-H.
2. Teddy reads clearly as a plush bear with improved face/seam/detail.
3. Formal shoe reads clearly as a dress shoe with sole/heel/tongue/laces rather than a block.
4. Passport reads as a layered passport/book with recognizable cover/page/spine treatment.
5. Suitcases/backpacks show construction detail beyond recolor.
6. Small collectibles remain readable at showcase scale 0.30.
7. ANOMALY/SECRET visual accents are restrained and do not disclose canon answers.
8. Index/showcase ownership, rarity and serial metadata remain unchanged.
9. Mobile performance remains acceptable with five displayed items.
10. Exact-source deploy receipt is required before claiming LIVE.
