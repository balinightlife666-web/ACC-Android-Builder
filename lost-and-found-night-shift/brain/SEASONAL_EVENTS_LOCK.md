# LOST & FOUND: NIGHT SHIFT — SEASONAL EVENTS LOCK v1.0

Status: ACTIVE LIVE-SERVICE AUTHORITY

## Purpose
Seasonal real-world events must create meaningful collectible demand and visible world changes, not merely temporary UI banners.

## 2026 priority calendar
- Halloween 2026 — real-world anchor: 31 October 2026.
- Christmas 2026 — real-world anchor: 25 December 2026.
- Exact in-game event start/end windows remain TBD until production scheduling is approved.

## Mandatory seasonal package
Each major seasonal event must include:
1. a limited seasonal collectible pool;
2. unique event-edition serial identity;
3. room/environment visual transformation appropriate to the event;
4. at least one event-specific gameplay/case hook or anomaly variation;
5. collection/index presentation that clearly distinguishes the edition;
6. preservation of core movement, inspection flow, Archive, trading integrity, and mobile readability.

## Asset rule
- Seasonal visuals are built as Roblox 3D/procedural assets and in-engine lighting/material/decor changes.
- Do not require AI-generated image assets for gameplay UI or environmental dressing unless Arda explicitly requests image generation later.
- Existing premium room visual language remains the base; seasonal dressing must feel integrated rather than replacing the whole map.

## Serial / edition rule
Default permanent Season 1 serials remain `<PREFIX>-S1-<NUMBER>`.
Seasonal editions use a visible edition token while retaining immutable server-authoritative ownership.

Examples of display format:
- Halloween 2026: `<PREFIX>-HW26-<GLOBAL MINT NUMBER>`
- Christmas 2026: `<PREFIX>-XMAS26-<GLOBAL MINT NUMBER>`

Rules:
- event serials are minted only by the server;
- edition is immutable after mint;
- event ownership remains instance-based and trade-compatible once M4-B is safely enabled;
- exact mint caps / drop rates are TBD and must be economy-reviewed before launch;
- event expiration may stop new minting, but already-owned instances remain owned and collectible unless a higher authority explicitly changes this.

## Halloween 2026 direction
Visual direction:
- premium dark airport back-office base retained;
- controlled Halloween dressing using in-engine 3D props, lighting, signage treatment, and anomaly atmosphere;
- no clutter that blocks SCAN / TAG / OPEN / decision consoles.

Collectible direction:
- limited Halloween-edition lost property and anomaly items;
- exact item names, models, rarities, mint caps, and case links remain TBD until content production.

## Christmas 2026 direction
Visual direction:
- premium airport lost-property room retained;
- restrained Christmas dressing using in-engine 3D props, warm practical lighting, parcel/gift visual language, and seasonal accents;
- maintain mystery tone rather than turning the experience into a generic holiday lobby.

Collectible direction:
- limited Christmas-edition lost property and anomaly items;
- exact item names, models, rarities, mint caps, and case links remain TBD until content production.

## Economy / collector intent
Seasonal items should create legitimate collector prestige through:
- time-bounded availability;
- unique serial provenance;
- distinct visual models/variants;
- rarity and discovery difficulty;
- future player-to-player trading once secure trading is enabled.

Do not add AFK/automatic farming merely to increase event engagement. Scarcity must remain server-authoritative.

## Production order
1. Complete M4-B secure trading foundation.
2. Improve core collectible 3D visual quality.
3. Prepare Halloween 2026 content early enough for runtime QC before the event.
4. Prepare Christmas 2026 content after Halloween foundation is reusable.
5. Use event telemetry to tune later seasonal drops rather than guessing permanent economy values.
