# LOST & FOUND: NIGHT SHIFT — GAME BIBLE v1.0

## Premise
The player works late shifts inside a transport hub Lost & Found department. Most items are ordinary. Some records contradict the objects in front of the player. Some claimants lie. Some claimants should no longer exist.

The employer initially appears to be a normal terminal Lost & Found office. Long-term lore reveals a deeper organization provisionally named the **Lost Property Authority (LPA)**, which contains objects and cases that should not exist.

## Tone
- premium stylized Roblox
- mysterious and tense
- light supernatural horror
- no gore dependency
- commercial/readable rather than oppressive
- normal workplace routine contrasted with impossible cases

## Player role
The player is an operational clerk/inspector, not a paranormal superhero.

The player's power comes from procedure, evidence, equipment, observation, and judgment.

## Core evidence channels
M0:
- scanner data
- claim tag
- physical/open inspection
- claimant identity

Future channels may include UV, fingerprint, X-ray, sound, database history, CCTV, and anomaly tools.

## Decisions
### RETURN
Give the item to a verified claimant.

### STORE
Keep normal unclaimed property in Lost & Found storage.

### QUARANTINE
Contain an item with impossible, anomalous, contaminated, or unsafe evidence.

### SECURITY
Escalate a dangerous person, theft attempt, identity incident, or immediate operational threat.

## Result grades
### PERFECT
Correct outcome after sufficient evidence collection.

### CORRECT
Correct outcome, but the player acted with incomplete evidence.

### QUESTIONABLE
A defensible/safe alternative, but not the preferred procedure.

### WRONG
Incorrect operational decision.

### CATASTROPHIC
A high-risk case was released or mishandled in a way that can trigger a server incident.

## Progression direction
Long-term ranks:
- Trainee
- Clerk
- Senior Clerk
- Inspector
- Supervisor
- Night Manager
- Archivist

M0 only tracks XP and Credits; rank unlock implementation comes later.

## Economy direction
Primary currency: Credits.  
Secondary future currency: Evidence Tokens.

Do not introduce currency clutter.

## Collection direction
Items can later have rarity and variants. Collection is about discovery and display, not only numerical power.

Rarity direction:
- Common
- Uncommon
- Rare
- Epic
- Legendary
- Mythic
- Secret
- Anomaly

Mutation/variant examples are visual/data variants of approved base assets, not excuses to generate uncontrolled new geometry.

## Season 1 mystery
### Flight 000
A flight number repeatedly appears in records even though the expected flight record is missing or impossible.

### The Ownerless Suitcase
A suitcase can enter the system with no valid owner and later reappear despite containment.

### The Lost Child
A child claimant/missing-person record is linked to a date many years in the past. Operationally the player must protect/escalate the child rather than solve the supernatural mystery on the spot.

These three pillars are connected, but the exact final explanation is **CANON UNKNOWN** at v1.0. Do not invent a final answer without Arda approval.

## Lore status terms
- CANON — approved fact.
- CANON UNKNOWN — intentionally unanswered.
- PROPOSED — idea only, not active truth.
- QUARANTINED — rejected/obsolete continuity.

- amber operational lighting
- cool scanner accents
- red quarantine/security accents

Do not spend M0 effort on a giant exterior airport.
